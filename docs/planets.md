# Planets details

## Overworld

### World gen
- normal overworld surface
- iron, copper, coal, zinc -> in disc patches
- gold, diamond, emerald, redstone -> vanilla ore gen


## Vulcanus

### World gen
- tungsten -> in disc patches (auto mining requires big miners craftable only on vulcanus)
- coal in disc patches
- calcite in disc patches
- huge lava lakes that can be processed into iron/copper (need a way to treat lava as infinite)
- sulfuric acid geysers

### Special features
- foundries and big miners can be crafted only on Vulcanus
- water <- steam condensation <- acid neutralisation (sulfuric acid + calcite)
- stone = lava processing
- concrete = molten iron + stone + water in a foundry
- heavy oil = coal liquefaction (custom recipe)


## Fulgora

### World gen
- flat island world with petroleum oceans (no water)
- scrap patches in small and medium sized islands

## Special features
- scrap processing: iron gear wheel, solid fuel, ice, steel plate, concrete (?), battery, stone, copper cable, red and blue circuits, LDS (?), holmium ore
- water from ice, copper/iron plates from iron gear, battery, green circuit, copper cables, LDS
- electric storms during the night which damage blocks and can be harnessed by lightning rods (or custom blocks) and stored in battery farms
- electromagnetic plants only craftable on Fulgora


## Gleba

### World gen
- similar to overworld with high frequency of swamps
- jelly stem -> jelly nuts
- yumako tree -> yumako
- iron/copper stromatolites (naturally generated in patches) -> iron/copper bacteria + stone
- enemies (pentapods) TBD

### Special features
- bioflux = jelly + yumako mash in biochambers
- biosulfur  = spoilage + bioflux in biochambers
- bioplastic = yumako mash + bioflux in biochambers
- biolubricant = jelly processing in biochambers
- bio rocket fuel = bioflux + jelly + water in biochambers
- iron/copper bacteria spoil quickly into iron and copper ore and can be reproduced with a dedicated processing chain in biochambers
- biochambers, special soil can only be crafted on Gleba
- some items/blocks requiring fast spoiling substrates are hard to craft on other planets (TBD)


## Aquilo

### World gen
- freezing ocean world of liquid ammonia
- lithium brine, fluorine, crude oil sources
- floating iceberg islands

### Special features
- cryogenic plant, fusion generator, fusion reactor, quantum processor can only be crafted on Aquilo
- ammoniacal solution, ammonia can only be produced and processed on Aquilo
